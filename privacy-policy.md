# Privacy Policy: Coding Keyboard for Programming

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Coding Keyboard never collects what you type. The free version uses Google services for ads, usage statistics and crash reports. The Adfree version collects nothing and has no internet access.

## What we never collect

This applies to both the free and Adfree versions. The keyboard never sends us, or anyone else:

- Anything you type, including words, passwords and messages
- Your keystrokes or key presses
- Your clipboard contents or clipboard history
- Which apps or websites you type in, or anything about the text fields you use

The app is built so that none of this can be included in the data described below.

## What the free version collects

The free version (Coding Keyboard) collects the data below to show ads, count how the app is used, and fix crashes. The Adfree version collects none of it.

| Data | Examples | Why | Received by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| App usage | Setup steps completed, which keyboard features you have tried, settings you choose (layout, size, theme, sound, vibration), whether the keyboard was used that day | Learn which features help people and where setup is confusing | Google Analytics for Firebase |
| Device and app info | Phone model, Android version, app version, language | Make sure the keyboard works on the devices people use | Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Google AdMob |
| Approximate location | Country or region, worked out from your IP address. Never GPS. | Usage statistics by region; ads suited to your region | Google Analytics for Firebase, Google AdMob |
| Identifiers | A random app-instance ID, and your device's advertising ID | Count users without knowing who they are; show and measure ads | Google Analytics for Firebase, Google AdMob |
| Crash reports | What the app was doing when it crashed or froze, device state | Find and fix bugs | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Ad interactions | Ads shown and tapped | Show ads and measure them | Google AdMob |

We do not sell this data. It is sent over encrypted connections. We see only totals and trends in Google's dashboards, not your identity.

## Third-party services

The free version uses these Google services. Each handles data under its own privacy terms.

- **Google Analytics for Firebase**: usage statistics. See [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: crash reports. See [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Google AdMob**: banner ads. See [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [how Google uses data for advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- **Google Play In-App Review**: may ask you to rate the app. Ratings go to Google Play; we never see who left them.

Google's own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) also applies. The Adfree version uses only Google Play In-App Review, which needs no data from the app.

## Data that stays on your device

Some data is saved only on your phone so the keyboard works the way you set it. We never receive it.

- Your keyboard settings, such as layout, theme, size, sound and vibration
- Clipboard history: up to 20 recent copied items plus any you pin, so you can paste them again
- Which features and tips you have already tried

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your device's backup to your own Google account. That backup belongs to you; we cannot access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data from your phone.

## Consent and your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the free version asks for your consent before it shows personalised ads or collects usage statistics and crash reports. If you decline, those stay off; you may still see non-personalised ads.

- **Change your choice:** open the app's menu and tap **Privacy settings**.
- **Limit ad tracking:** reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings, under Privacy, then Ads.
- **Stop all collection:** use the Adfree version, which has no internet access, or uninstall the free version.

## Retention and your rights

Usage statistics are kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, then deleted by Google. Advertising data is kept under Google's own retention rules.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. The data is linked to a random ID, not your name, so we usually cannot find it without that ID. Email us and we will help as far as we can; uninstalling the app also resets its app-instance ID.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, we rely on your consent for usage statistics, crash reports and personalised ads. You can withdraw it at any time in **Privacy settings**.

## Children

Coding Keyboard is a programming tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
